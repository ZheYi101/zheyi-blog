# Choosing college preferences——decide to learn Computer Major —— 24.7 - 24.9
At start of the my scheduling time of major and college, after Chinese National College Entrance Examination, I spent some time to decide what major I want to learn, and willing to work with it(also able to earn much money). Then it comes out to be Computer Major.

After that, I begin to search for information about those colleges which I'm capable enough to apply. After ten more days' deep research, I decided to apply Zhejiang University of Technology(ZJUT, which I am currently in), despite my score of Chinese National College Entrance Examination is roughly enough to get me into a non-popular degree programme(certainly excludes Computer Major).So it's really unlikely for me to directly get into it.

If u r family with me, u should know that I have got a Change of Degree Programme. Yes, in fact, at the point of choosing college preferences, I have researched about the practicability of it, in the way of seeing posts in Baidu Tieba, talking in Fresher's group of zjut with seniors, and reading relevant official document. I'm totally a daydreamer(lol).

So days later submission of college preference form, just as I "hoped", I got the chemistry programme offer, and finally successfully changed my degree programme at the end of first academic year(I also wrote a blog about it).

By the way, on that vacation, I have learnt 2 months C++ and some algorithm. One reason is certainly for my future Major Change Exam, the other is that I considered preparing for ACM Contest at that time.

As we all know.In china, a college usually have four post-graduation pathways

1. Taking the National Postgraduate Entrance Examination(needs you learn hard in the last two years of college life)
2. Postgraduate recommendation(a normal policy in Chinese college, needs you work hard the whole college life to compete with others for the recommended places)
3. Studying abroad(usually means a large amount of money cost)
4. Direct employment

> At this period, I think there's `60%` percent for me to directly get employed, `30%` take the postgraduate entrance exam, and `10%`for the left two.


OK, remember this percentage distribution, my readers. After a period it will change a lot. This is what reveals my consideration in different period on future schedule most.
